# WS15 restore handoff

Decision 47 keeps the external credential epoch, post-restore schedule hold and
directional schema errors. Startup and offline preparation share the trusted
credential-file boundary and persistent epoch directory; Helm uses a dedicated
retained volume. Acknowledgment discards past schedule instants without a provider
gate. The restore drill backs up a normally operating store, revokes through the
supported API, and checks actual schedule admission before and after acknowledgment.

Payment recovery is manual; follow the operator runbook kept outside this repository.
