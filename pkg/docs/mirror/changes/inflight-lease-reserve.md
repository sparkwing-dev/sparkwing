### Changed

- **controller:** A team over its request budget keeps its running jobs alive
  When `--requests-per-token-minute` refuses a team, a reserve of one eighth of that budget (at least one request), on top of it, still admits lease renewals, node transitions and step start and finish from a claim or eval credential whose lease is current. Running jobs now slow down instead of losing their lease or their result. Snapshot reads, claim polls, log writes, console requests, publisher credentials and result replays sent after the lease ended never use the reserve; a live lease resending a result it already recorded does. For a named team with a budget of at least 8, the sustained ceiling becomes 9/8 of the budget and the worst one-minute window 9/4 of it instead of 2 times. Credentials of the standalone default team each have their own bucket, so that team could already exceed the budget before this change. An in-flight write the budget refuses adds no debt to it, so retrying nodes cannot hold the team's own budget at zero. Requests the reserve admits are counted in `sparkwing_inflight_reserve_admitted_total`, and a refusal from the spent reserve is counted as `inflight_reserve` in `sparkwing_principal_throttled_total`.
- **runner:** A rate-limited job result is retried until it is recorded
  A node transition the controller refuses with `rate_limited`, including the final result, now retries after its Retry-After plus jitter until it is recorded, the lease ends, or one lease length passes after the first refusal; that deadline also cuts off a request still in flight. The host owns these retries, and the client no longer retries a rate-limited node transition itself. It previously gave up after the client's four attempts and lost the result.
- **runner:** Lease renewals and node writes no longer read state first
  A node's lease renewal, and an owner's renewal and transitions for a node it adopted, no longer read the node or re-authenticate before the write, so an empty team budget cannot refuse the read and leave the write unsent. The controller still checks the claim fence on every write.
- **client:** Rate-limited requests retry with jitter above Retry-After
  Callers refused together no longer all return in the same instant.
