### Security

- **controller:** Limit each account to three owned teams that are unpaid in the current payment mode
  Every team carries its own free storage, download and request allowances, so creating teams without limit multiplied them. The limit is checked when an account creates a team, accepts an owner invitation or is promoted to owner of an unpaid team, and the refusal is `422 limit_exceeded` with reason `unpaid_team_limit`. Taking ownership of a team that has paid is always allowed. To own another unpaid team, add funds to one of your unpaid teams or delete or leave one until you own fewer than three. Teams already owned above the limit, for example after a refund, keep working and keep their allowances. Operator-created teams are not checked when created. See [team resource limits](docs/limits.md#team-resource-limits).
