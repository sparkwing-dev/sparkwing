### Added

- **cli + controller:** Reissue the administrator credential of a team nobody signs in to
  `sparkwing operator teams reissue-admin --team SLUG` and `POST /api/v1/operator/teams/{team}/admin-credentials` issue a fresh team-admin credential under the same operator authority as `operator teams create`, with the same owner grants and 30-day lifetime. A monitoring team made with `operator teams create` previously had no way to outlive its first credential, because nobody can sign in as its account and an admin credential cannot extend itself. Teams with any member who has an email or a sign-in identity are refused with `conflict`. `--revoke-previous` also revokes the earlier administrator credentials and every token and session derived from them in the same transaction. The issue and each revocation are audited with the operator as actor.
